# Gikomba Market Stall Performance Analysis

**Name:** Siwule Abiye Assefa  
**Cohort:** PLP Data Analytics - Week 1  

## Business Problem Summary
Wanjiku operates a fresh-produce stall at Gikomba Market and recorded two weeks of daily sales and rough inventory figures manually. As a freelance data analyst, this analysis evaluates her sales trajectory, identifies peak vs. slow periods, determines weekend versus weekday performance patterns, and highlights stock items requiring immediate replenishment to prevent revenue loss.


## Top Three Findings
1. **Strong Growth Trajectory:** Total sales increased from KES 21,500 in Week 1 to KES 24,400 in Week 2, reflecting a positive week-on-week growth of 13.49%.
2. **Weekend Sales Dominance:** Average weekend sales (KES 4,725.00) significantly outperformed average weekday sales (KES 2,630.00), showing an 79.66% increase during weekends.
3. **Inventory Risks:** Both **Tomatoes** (18 kg in stock vs. 25 kg reorder level) and **Sukuma** (9 kg in stock vs. 15 kg reorder level) are critically low and require immediate restocking.
## Recommendations for Wanjiku
1. **Prioritize Weekend Stocking:** Given that weekend sales are 79.66% higher than weekdays (averaging KES 4,725.00 compared to KES 2,630.00), Wanjiku should ensure her stall is fully stocked by Friday evening to capture peak customer traffic.
2. **Immediate Restocking of Tomatoes and Sukuma:** With inventory levels for Tomatoes (18 kg) and Sukuma (9 kg) falling below their respective reorder thresholds (25 kg and 15 kg), immediate wholesale purchases are necessary to avoid stockouts.
3. **Introduce Weekday Promotions:** To bridge the gap between slow weekdays (averaging KES 2,630.00) and lucrative weekends, Wanjiku should introduce bundle discounts or promotional pricing from Monday to Thursday to stimulate weekday demand.
